COMING SOON

        

          We're Building Our Local Vendor Network         

        

          Great events are the result of great people working together.         

        

          As Hayden Mill Events prepares to welcome its first celebrations,           we're getting to know the talented event professionals serving           Tecumseh and the surrounding communities.         

        

          From caterers and photographers to florists, DJs, bartenders,           bakers, rental companies, and other event professionals, our goal           is to develop relationships with businesses that understand our           venue and share our commitment to creating memorable events.
