HAYDEN MILL EVENTS

        

          Preferred Partners         

        

          We're building a network of talented local professionals           to help make every Hayden Mill event exceptional.         

      
    
  
     
    
      
        

          Great events are the result of great people working together.         

        

          As Hayden Mill Events prepares to welcome its first           celebrations, we're getting to know the talented event           professionals serving Tecumseh and the surrounding communities.         

        

          From caterers and photographers to florists, DJs, bartenders,           bakers, rental companies, planners, and other event professionals,           our goal is to develop relationships with businesses that           understand our venue and share our commitment to creating           memorable events.         

        

          Rather than simply publishing a list of businesses, we want           our Preferred Partners collection to become a genuinely useful           planning resource built around professionals we've had an           opportunity to get to know.         

        

                       Our Preferred Partners list is coming soon.                    

        

          We're Building Our Local Vendor Network         

        

          One of the advantages of a great event venue is becoming part           of a larger community of people who know how to make events           happen.         

        

          As Hayden Mill gets started, we're intentionally taking some           time to get to know the professionals who serve Tecumseh and           the surrounding area.         

        

          We want to learn about their businesses, see their work,           understand the services they provide, and eventually build           relationships with professionals who know Hayden Mill too.         

        

          Over time, this page will grow into a resource for couples,           families, businesses, and organizations planning events at           the Mill.

          Why We're Not Rushing the List         

        

          It's easy to create a page filled with company names.         

        

          We'd rather create something more useful.         

        

          As Hayden Mill begins hosting events, we'll have opportunities           to see different vendors work in the space, learn how they           approach their events, and develop relationships with local           professionals.         

        

          That experience will help us build a Preferred Partners           collection that can actually help future clients with their           planning.         

        

                       We think that's worth taking a little time to do well.                    

                 
          

COMING SOON

          

            A Local Resource for Planning Your Event           

          

            As our network develops, we'll begin adding event             professionals to this page.           

          

            Our goal is to make it easier for Hayden Mill clients to             discover businesses that can help with the many pieces of             planning an event.           

          

            That might mean finding a caterer for dinner, a photographer             who understands the building's historic character, a florist             who can help transform the room, a DJ who can keep a reception             moving, or one of the many other professionals who help turn             an idea into an event.           

          

                           Check back as our Preferred Partners network grows.                        

        
        

          Are You an Event Professional?         

        

          We're also interested in hearing from event professionals           serving Tecumseh and the surrounding communities.         

        

          If you're a caterer, photographer, florist, DJ, bartender,           baker, planner, rental company, videographer, or provide another           service that helps create great events, we'd love to get to           know your business.         

        

          Tell us a little about what you do, the areas you serve, the           types of events you specialize in, and where we can see more           of your work.         

        

          As Hayden Mill's event calendar grows, we're looking forward           to building strong relationships with the professionals who           will be working alongside us.

          Planning an Event at Hayden Mill?         

        

          You don't need to have every vendor selected before you begin           planning your event.         

        

          Start with the big picture: what you're celebrating, how many           people you'd like to invite, and the kind of experience you'd           like to create.         

        

          The rest can come together from there.
