          Preferred Partners         

        

          We're building a network of talented local professionals           to help make every Hayden Mill event exceptional.         

      
    
  
     
    
      
        

          Great events are the result of great people working together.         

        

          As Hayden Mill Events prepares to welcome its first celebrations,           we're getting to know the talented event professionals serving           Tecumseh and the surrounding communities.         

        

          From caterers and photographers to florists, DJs, bartenders,           bakers, rental companies, planners, and other event professionals,           our goal is to develop relationships with businesses that understand           our venue and share our commitment to creating memorable events.         

        

          Rather than simply publishing a list of businesses, we want our           Preferred Partners collection to become a useful planning resource           built around professionals we know and trust.         

        

                       Our Preferred Partners list is coming soon.                    

        

          Building the Right Local Network         

        

          Choosing the right professionals can make an enormous difference           in how smoothly an event comes together.         

        

          That's why we don't want our Preferred Partners page to simply be           a directory of businesses in the area. We want to take the time to           get to know local event professionals, understand the services they           provide, and develop relationships with people who work well with           both our venue and our clients.         

        

          As Hayden Mill begins hosting events, we'll continue meeting and           working with professionals throughout Tecumseh and the surrounding           communities.         

        

          Over time, this page will grow into a resource for clients looking           for professionals across many areas of event planning.

          Why We're Taking Our Time         

        

          A recommendation from a venue should mean something.         

        

          We don't want to call a business a Preferred Partner simply because           they're nearby or because we've found their name online.         

        

          We want to become familiar with the people behind the business,           understand how they work, and develop relationships with           professionals who appreciate the importance of communication,           preparation, and great service.         

        

          We also want our partners to become familiar with Hayden Mill.           Knowing the venue, understanding event flow, and being comfortable           working alongside other vendors can make setup and event day much           easier for everyone involved.         

        

                       So rather than rushing to fill this page, we're building our             network thoughtfully.                    

        

          Are You a Local Event Professional?         

        

          We'd like to hear from you.         

        

          If you're a caterer, photographer, florist, DJ, bartender, planner,           baker, rental company, videographer, or other event professional           serving Tecumseh and the surrounding area, introduce yourself.
